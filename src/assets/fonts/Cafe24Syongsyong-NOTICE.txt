Cafe24Syongsyong-subset.woff2 in this directory is a MODIFIED subset of Cafe24Syongsyong-v2.0.ttf
(glyphs for the header support-widget label "커피 한 잔 후원하기", Basic Latin, and ☕),

Regenerate (e.g. after changing the label text; needs `pip install fonttools brotli`):
  pyftsubset Cafe24Syongsyong-v2.0.ttf --text="<label text>" --unicodes="U+0020-007E" \
    --flavor=woff2 --layout-features='*' --no-hinting --output-file=Cafe24Syongsyong-subset.woff2
